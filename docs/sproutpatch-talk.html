Sproutpatch

You say how you feel. It makes you touch grass.

A local-first daily check-in. Gemma talks. TypeScript sends you outside.

The problem

Most “how are you” apps keep you on the screen.

  • They chat. They store your words on a server.
  • “Go outside” is a generic line that ignores rain, heat, and sunset.
  • A check-in should end in a walk, not another conversation.

The idea

The screen is the shortest part.

1. Check inA few lines, or tap a mood.
2. Short replyYour local model writes one or two sentences.
3. One walkCode picks where and how long, from real weather.
4. Timer, then I'm backA sprout in History for every walk you finish.

Demo

Write a note. Start a walk.

The contract

Gemma may talk. Code decides the rest.

The model only returns JSON: a reply, a mood score, themes, and a risk flag. It can raise the alarm. It can never pick the weather, the walk, or the length.

Safety

The keyword check runs first.

  • Before Ollama. Before the on-device model. Before any plugin.
  • Risk mode: no model, no nudge, a support card. In India that is Tele-MANAS at 14416.
  • It stores a mood score and a risk marker. Never the text.

Nudges

Real weather. Mood sets the minutes.

  • Open-Meteo, no API key. Coordinates rounded to about 1 km.
  • Rain or storms → five minutes at the doorstep.
  • Heat → shorter, shaded. Night → a lit street.
  • Mood still sets the length: 10, 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes.

Look and sound

Day, night, and your own music.

  • Theme follows the clock: light 6:30–18:30, dark after. Or pin it.
  • The grass scene follows too: sun, moon, fireflies.
  • Meadow and Night Grass are generated piano. No audio files shipped.
  • My music: add ogg or mp3, tap a song, it stays on that device.

Bring your own model

Ollama, the phone, or no model at all.

  • Any Ollama tag on your computer. Gemma is preferred.
  • Gemma 3 270M in the browser, on the phone GPU, stored in OPFS.
  • Or an OpenAI-compatible server. Continue without a model and it still works.

Privacy

The words never leave the machine.

No account. No API keys in the app. Entries live in IndexedDB. Export JSON or delete everything in History. The only network calls are your local model, if you have one, and Open-Meteo.
